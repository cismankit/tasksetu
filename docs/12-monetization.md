# Monetization

## Philosophy
Free tier must solve real pain for students and families. Paid tiers unlock convenience, family scale, and business tools — not basic government prep checklists.

## Tiers (proposed)

### Free
- 1 user profile
- 2 family members
- 20 documents
- All national + 1 regional pack
- Local reminders
- Rule-based recommendations

### Plus (₹99–149/month)
- Unlimited family members
- Unlimited documents
- Document pack exports (PDF)
- Priority regional templates
- Cloud sync across devices

### Business (₹299/month)
- Receipt categorization + monthly summary
- CSV export
- Multiple shop profiles
- WhatsApp reminder templates

## Revenue timing
- **MVP:** Free only, validate retention
- **Beta:** Waitlist for Plus, no billing
- **v1:** Razorpay subscription (India)

## Alternative revenue (long-term)
- Affiliate-free official link directory
- B2B white-label for NGOs/educational institutions
- Verified regional pack marketplace (creator revenue share)

## What we won't monetize
- Selling user document data
- Predatory ads on sensitive flows
- Paywalling income certificate checklists for low-income students
