# User Personas

## 1. Priya — College Student (MP)
- **Age:** 19 | **Location:** Bhopal
- **Goals:** Scholarship applications, admission docs, exam admin
- **Pain:** Doesn't know which certificates are valid this year; misses deadlines
- **TaskSetu value:** MP scholarship pack, income certificate checklist, reminders

## 2. Ramesh — Parent & Family Manager
- **Age:** 42 | **Location:** Indore
- **Goals:** School admission for two kids, family document organization
- **Pain:** Documents scattered; wife asks on WhatsApp repeatedly
- **TaskSetu value:** Family profiles, document packs, WhatsApp share helper

## 3. Sunita — Kirana Shopkeeper
- **Age:** 38 | **Location:** Ujjain
- **Goals:** Track UPI receipts, rent proofs, shop license renewal
- **Pain:** Month-end accounting is manual screenshots
- **TaskSetu value:** Receipt organizer, monthly admin pack, reminders

## 4. Govind — Farmer
- **Age:** 55 | **Location:** Rural MP
- **Goals:** Scheme eligibility documents, income certificate
- **Pain:** Tehsil visits without complete document set
- **TaskSetu value:** Income certificate prep, Hindi UI, simple steps

## 5. Arjun — Gig Worker (Swiggy/Zomato)
- **Age:** 26 | **Location:** Gwalior
- **Goals:** PAN, bank docs, payment records
- **Pain:** Platform asks for docs he doesn't have organized
- **TaskSetu value:** ID pack, missing doc alerts

## 6. Asha — Senior Citizen
- **Age:** 68 | **Location:** Jabalpur
- **Goals:** Medicine reminders, insurance renewal
- **Pain:** Complex apps; relies on children
- **TaskSetu value:** Large text (future), family-assigned docs, medicine reminders

## 7. Neha — Busy Professional
- **Age:** 31 | **Location:** Remote / MP native
- **Goals:** Manage parents' admin from afar
- **Pain:** Cannot visit Tehsil; needs shareable checklists
- **TaskSetu value:** Family manager mode, status tracker, WhatsApp share
