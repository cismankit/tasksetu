# TaskSetu Vision

## One-liner
**TaskSetu** is an adaptive life-admin app that helps people manage boring, repetitive, confusing, manual, and scattered daily tasks — without turning into a generic chatbot.

## Tagline
- Global: *Your life admin, handled.*
- India: *Kaam ho simple. Life ho sorted.*

## The problem we solve
Across India (and globally), life admin is fragmented:
- Government forms live on different portals with changing requirements
- Documents sit in WhatsApp, email, and physical folders
- Deadlines are remembered mentally or lost in chat threads
- Families coordinate admin through informal messages
- Small business owners track UPI receipts manually
- Students juggle admission, scholarship, and exam paperwork repeatedly

People do not want another AI that *answers questions*. They want something that **guides them through the work**.

## Product workflow
TaskSetu moves users through a structured loop:

**Need → Checklist → Document Pack → Reminder → Apply → Track → Follow-up → Store Proof**

## What TaskSetu is
- A smart checklist app for real-life tasks
- A private document vault with expiry awareness
- A reminder and follow-up system
- A regional task template engine (India-first, MP pilot)
- A family admin dashboard
- A rule-based recommendation layer (AI optional, modular)

## What TaskSetu is not
- Not a government portal or impersonation tool
- Not an Aadhaar/PAN verification service
- Not a bank/UPI data scraper in MVP
- Not a generic ChatGPT wrapper

## Success metrics (12-month horizon)
- Weekly active users completing at least one checklist step
- Document vault adoption (≥3 docs per active user)
- Reminder completion rate
- Regional template usage (MP pack engagement)
- Beta waitlist → activated user conversion

## Long-term north star
Become the default **life-admin operating system** for households and small businesses in India — calm, trustworthy, and region-aware.
