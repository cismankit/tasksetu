# User Interview Questions

## Screening (5 min)
1. What city/district do you live in?
2. Which describes you best: student, parent, shopkeeper, other?
3. How do you currently track important documents?

## Pain discovery (15 min)
4. Tell me about the last time you needed a government certificate. What was hard?
5. How do you remember deadlines for scholarships, fees, or renewals?
6. Where do you store photos of Aadhaar, marksheets, receipts?
7. How does your family ask each other for documents?
8. Have you ever missed a deadline because paperwork wasn't ready?

## Product reaction (10 min)
9. [Show MP income certificate checklist] What's missing? What's wrong?
10. Would you trust an app to store document photos? Why/why not?
11. Hindi vs English — which do you prefer for this app?
12. Would you use WhatsApp share for missing doc lists?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
13. Would you join a free beta for MP users?
14. Can we follow up in 2 weeks?

## Log template
- Date, persona, region, top 3 pains, template feedback, beta interest (Y/N)
