# Competitor Notes

## DigiLocker
- **Strength:** Government-issued documents
- **Gap:** No task workflows, reminders, family admin, regional checklists

## Google Keep / Notes
- **Strength:** Ubiquitous
- **Gap:** No structure, no regional intelligence

## Notion / Todoist
- **Strength:** Flexible tasks
- **Gap:** User must build own system; no India gov templates

## ChatGPT / Gemini
- **Strength:** Answers questions
- **Gap:** No persistent vault, reminders, status tracking; hallucination risk on procedures

## Khatabook / payment apps
- **Strength:** Business receipts
- **Gap:** Not life-admin holistic; no document packs

## mParivahan / UMANG
- **Strength:** Official services
- **Gap:** Service-specific, not personal admin OS

## TaskSetu differentiation
Structured workflows + regional packs + family + privacy-first vault — AI optional.
