# India User Pain Points

## Document chaos
- Aadhaar/PAN photos repeated across WhatsApp chats
- No single expiry view for passport, license, insurance
- Family members ask for same document multiple times

## Government form confusion
- Income/caste/domicile requirements differ by scheme
- e-District portals vary by state; users don't know where to start
- Field verification visits without complete file

## Deadline anxiety
- Scholarship and admission dates scattered across websites
- No unified reminder system tied to document readiness

## Payment/receipt tracking
- Shopkeepers save UPI screenshots inconsistently
- School fees and medical bills lost in gallery scroll

## Follow-up burden
- Application status checked manually on portals
- No structured "next follow-up date" habit

## Digital literacy gap
- First-gen users fear wrong submission
- Hindi/regional language UI often missing on gov sites

## WhatsApp as default OS
- Families coordinate via forwards, not structured lists
- TaskSetu meets users where they are with share helpers
